Welcome to PlainRouter. This guide takes you from a new account to an active first-party Signals pixel.

<Card title="What you will accomplish" icon="circle-check">
  Create your account, confirm your workspace, connect a first-party collection domain, and verify the Signals pixel on your live website.
</Card>

## Before you begin

Before you begin, you need:

- An email address you can verify.
- Access to your website's shared `<head>` template or site-wide code settings.
- Access to the DNS provider for your website domain.

## Set up your account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    [Create a PlainRouter account](https://plainrouter.com/register), then verify your email address.

    PlainRouter creates a personal team and a default workspace for you. A workspace keeps one client, brand, or market and its connected resources together.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name your workspace">
    Open the [PlainRouter dashboard](https://plainrouter.com/dashboard), then go to **Settings** → **Workspace**.

    Give the workspace a name your team will recognize, such as the client, brand, or market name. You can create and switch workspaces later when you need to separate access or data.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Set up Signals

Signals collects first-party conversion events from your website through a collection subdomain you control.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start your first signal">
    In the dashboard, open **Signals** and click **New signal** or **Set up your first signal**.

    Enter a collection subdomain that you control, such as `collect.example.com`. Do not include `https://`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the DNS records">
    Copy the DNS records from PlainRouter into your DNS provider. Keep the setup screen open or return to it from **Signals**.

    DNS validation can take several minutes. If the records remain pending, click **Retry validation** after your provider has published them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Install the Signals pixel">
    When the collection domain becomes active, copy the generated snippet and add it immediately before the closing `</head>` tag on every page of your website.

    Deploy the change and visit the live site. The pixel sends an anonymous, cookieless `PageView`, and PlainRouter completes setup automatically after receiving it.

    See [Install the Signals pixel](/signals/install-pixel) for code placement and troubleshooting.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Signal setup is optional and resumable. Closing the setup screen does not remove your collection domain or log you out.
</Note>

## Confirm your setup

Your initial PlainRouter setup is complete when:

- **Signals** shows your signal as **Active** after receiving the first `PageView`.
- Your workspace name clearly identifies the client, brand, or market it contains.

You can now return to the [dashboard](https://plainrouter.com/dashboard) and continue working from the workspace you created.
