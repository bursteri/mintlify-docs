Welcome to PlainRouter. This guide takes you from a new account to your first working connection.

PlainRouter has two starting paths:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Connect an AI agent" icon="bot" href="/quickstart">
    Authorize Codex or another MCP-compatible client to work with an advertising account.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Collect website signals" icon="radio" href="/signals/install-pixel">
    Add a first-party collection domain and install the Signals pixel on your website.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

You can complete either path first. Set up both when you want your AI workflow and conversion measurement in the same workspace.

## Before you begin

Start with the access required for the path you plan to complete:

- An email address you can verify.
- Access to a supported advertising account for the AI-agent path.
- Access to your website and DNS provider for the Signals path.

## Set up your account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    [Create a PlainRouter account](https://plainrouter.com/register), then verify your email address.

    PlainRouter creates a personal team and a default workspace for you. A workspace keeps one client, brand, or market and its connected resources together.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name your workspace">
    Open the [PlainRouter dashboard](https://plainrouter.com/dashboard), then go to **Settings** → **Workspace**.

    Give the workspace a name your team will recognize, such as the client, brand, or market name. You can create and switch workspaces later when you need to separate access or data.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Connect your AI client

Use this path when you want to manage advertising work from Codex or another MCP-compatible client.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add PlainRouter to your client">
    In Codex, run:

    ```bash
    codex mcp add plainrouter --url https://plainrouter.com/mcp
    ```

    For another MCP client, add `https://plainrouter.com/mcp` as a remote server URL.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize an advertising account">
    Start a PlainRouter action from your client. When the browser authorization page opens, sign in and choose the advertising account this connection may access.

    PlainRouter scopes the connection to the account and permissions you approve.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the connection">
    Ask your agent to retrieve the connected advertising account context. Confirm that it returns the account you selected.

    See [Connect an AI agent](/quickstart) for the focused connection guide.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Set up Signals

Use this path when you want to collect first-party conversion events from your website.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start your first signal">
    In the dashboard, open **Signals** and click **New signal** or **Set up your first signal**.

    Enter a collection subdomain that you control, such as `collect.example.com`. Do not include `https://`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the DNS records">
    Copy the DNS records from PlainRouter into your DNS provider. Keep the setup screen open or return to it from **Signals**.

    DNS validation can take several minutes. If the records remain pending, click **Retry validation** after your provider has published them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Install the Signals pixel">
    When the collection domain becomes active, copy the generated snippet and add it immediately before the closing `</head>` tag on every page of your website.

    Deploy the change and visit the live site. The pixel sends an anonymous, cookieless `PageView`, and PlainRouter completes setup automatically after receiving it.

    See [Install the Signals pixel](/signals/install-pixel) for code placement and troubleshooting.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Signal setup is optional and resumable. Closing the setup screen does not remove your collection domain or log you out.
</Note>

## Confirm your setup

Your first PlainRouter session is complete when the parts you chose are working:

- Your AI client returns the advertising account you authorized.
- **Signals** shows your signal as **Active** after receiving the first `PageView`.
- Your workspace name clearly identifies the client, brand, or market it contains.

You can now return to the [dashboard](https://plainrouter.com/dashboard) and continue working from the workspace you created.
