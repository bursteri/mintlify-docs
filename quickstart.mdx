Set up Plainrouter Signals by creating a workspace, activating your collection domain, and installing the generated browser snippet. The browser setup is complete when Plainrouter stores the first qualifying arrival from your website. Connecting a Meta ad account is optional for this first step.

An arrival confirms website collection. Add your consent integration and connect a Meta dataset separately to deliver advertising conversion events.

<Info>
  [Registration is open](https://plainrouter.com/register). Review [pricing](https://plainrouter.com/pricing) for the Free band's spend threshold, arrival allowance, and overage rules.
</Info>

## Which setup path do I need?

| Your goal | Start here | Expected outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Measure website arrivals | Follow this quickstart. | An active collection domain and a stored real arrival. |
| Send consented conversions to Meta | Finish Signals setup, then [connect Meta](/signals/connect-meta). | A connected dataset and separately verified event delivery. |
| Add backend conversions | Use the [Conversion API](/api/conversions) or [choose an SDK](/sdk/overview). | A server event accepted by Plainrouter; inspect delivery afterward. |
| Connect an AI agent to a Meta ad account | Follow [MCP setup](/mcp/setup). | A client authenticated to the intended workspace and Meta ad account. |

## Before you begin

Before you begin, you need:

- An email address you can access.
- Access to the DNS provider for your website domain.
- Access to your website's shared `<head>` template or site-wide code injection setting.
- A consent integration for conversion events and advertising delivery. The identity-free arrival check does not wait for a consent grant.

## Set up your account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    [Create a Plainrouter account](https://plainrouter.com/register).

    Plainrouter creates a personal team and a default workspace, then opens the Signals setup flow. A workspace keeps one client, brand, or market and its connected resources together.

    Connecting an ad account is optional. Open **Connections** when you are ready to configure Meta reporting, Meta delivery, or other available provider connections. You can continue Signals installation without connecting Meta.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name your workspace">
    Open the [Plainrouter dashboard](https://plainrouter.com/dashboard), then go to **Settings** → **Workspace**.

    Give the workspace a name your team will recognize, such as the client, brand, or market name. You can create and switch workspaces later when you need to separate access or data.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Join an invited team

Open the invitation email, sign in or register with the invited email address, and choose **Join** on the invitation page. Opening the link alone does not add you to the team. If you are signed in with a different email, use the account-switch option and continue with the invited address.

Invitation links last seven days. After acceptance, Plainrouter opens the invited team's settings. Owners can resend or cancel pending invitations from team settings. Ask the owner to resend an expired or older link; accepted and cancelled invitations cannot be reused.

## Set up Signals

Signals collects first-party conversion events from your website through a collection subdomain you control.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter your website address">
    Open **Signals** and continue setup. In **Website address**, enter the website where you will install Signals, such as `www.example.com`.

    The form supplies `https://`. Use the exact website host, without a page path, query string, or fragment. The website address identifies the site allowed to send events; it is separate from your collection subdomain, such as `collect.example.com`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm your collection hostname on Install">
    Saving your website address opens **Install** directly. Meta authorization and dataset selection are optional and do not gate installation or arrival collection.

    Under **Choose your collection hostname**, keep the suggested `t` label or edit it, for example to `collect`. For `www.example.com`, the fixed domain suffix is `example.com`, giving `t.example.com` or `collect.example.com`. Click **Use t** or **Use collect** to confirm. Enter only the label, not a complete hostname, URL, or path.

    The hostname cannot be edited after provisioning. Copy the final hostname shown in the DNS instructions; if your choice is already allocated, Plainrouter can add a suffix. See [hostname rules](/signals/configure-dns#choose-your-collection-hostname).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the CNAME record">
    Copy the CNAME name and target from Plainrouter into your DNS provider. Keep the setup screen open or return to it from **Signals**.

    CNAME detection and automatic HTTPS validation can take several minutes. If setup remains pending, leave the record in place and return to **Install** after your provider has published the change.

    See [Configure DNS and CNAME](/signals/configure-dns) for provider field guidance and troubleshooting.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Install Signals and wait for an arrival">
    When the collection domain becomes active, copy the generated snippet into the shared `<head>` of your site, deploy, and visit a page. The snippet sends one identity-free arrival before evaluating consent.

    After installing, choose **I’ve added the CNAME and snippet** to record the setup path. This confirmation alone does not verify traffic. Plainrouter verifies the browser installation after the first qualifying arrival is stored for the workspace. The setup screen polls automatically, backs off while it waits, and resumes after you return to the tab. Connect your consent flow separately before sending conversion events or advertising identity.

    See [Install the Signals pixel](/signals/install-pixel) for the snippet and consent requirements.
  </Step>
</Steps>

For server-side API, SDK, or CLI access, the workspace owner manages the Signals secret under **Settings → API secret**. Follow [API secret rotation](/api/authentication#where-do-i-manage-the-signals-api-secret) to install and verify a replacement before confirming it. The secret is shown once. The browser snippet uses the publishable key and must never contain this secret.

Managed-hostname setup can also recognize qualifying verified server revenue. The identity-free `POST /api/v1/verification-events` operation remains available as a server-side diagnostic, but it does not count as real arrival or revenue evidence and does not complete onboarding. See [Test Signal ingestion](/signals/verify-ingestion).

<Note>
  Signal setup is optional and resumable. Closing the setup screen does not remove your collection domain or log you out.
</Note>

## Confirm your setup

Your initial Plainrouter setup is complete when:

- **Signals** shows setup as complete after receiving the first qualifying arrival on an active collection domain.
- Your workspace name clearly identifies the client, brand, or market it contains.

You can now return to the [dashboard](https://plainrouter.com/dashboard) and continue working from the workspace you created.

Before the workspace's first arrival, **Dashboard** and **Signals** show installation instructions directly. For a provisioned managed hostname, copy the CNAME and browser snippet there; you do not need arrival data to access them. The screens refresh while waiting for collection. Open **Signals → Install** for hostname selection and the full verification controls.

After the first arrival, use **Signals** for actual collection and reporting. The Dashboard no longer shows sample charts. See [billing setup](/billing/overview) to interpret a checkout notice.

## Why is setup still waiting?

| What you see | Check next |
| --- | --- |
| Collection domain is pending | Confirm the exact CNAME name and target, then [retry DNS and HTTPS validation](/signals/configure-dns#troubleshoot-validation). |
| Domain is active, but no arrival appears | Confirm the deployed page loads `v4.js` and sends an accepted `POST /v1/arrival`. Follow the [browser network checks](/signals/install-pixel#verify-the-installation). |
| The verification API returns `202`, but setup stays open | That response verifies the server write path. It does not replace a real arrival. See [which check proves what](/signals/verify-ingestion#which-check-do-i-need). |
| Arrivals appear, but Meta has no conversions | Arrivals never create Meta deliveries. Configure consent, send a conversion event, and [verify the Meta destination](/signals/connect-meta#how-do-i-verify-meta-delivery). |
| Dashboard or Signals shows installation instructions instead of reports | The workspace has not recorded its first arrival. Follow the displayed setup, deploy the snippet, and visit the website. A copied snippet or an ingestion diagnostic does not prove browser collection. |
