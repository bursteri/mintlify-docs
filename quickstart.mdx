Welcome to PlainRouter. This guide takes you from a new account to verified first-party Signals ingestion.

<Card title="What you will accomplish" icon="circle-check">
  Create your account, confirm your workspace, connect a first-party collection domain, and verify the server-side ingestion path.
</Card>

## Before you begin

Before you begin, you need:

- An email address you can verify.
- Access to the DNS provider for your website domain.
- A Signal tracker secret for API verification, or an authorized MCP connection with `signals.verify`.

## Set up your account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    [Create a PlainRouter account](https://plainrouter.com/register), then verify your email address.

    PlainRouter creates a personal team and a default workspace for you. A workspace keeps one client, brand, or market and its connected resources together.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name your workspace">
    Open the [PlainRouter dashboard](https://plainrouter.com/dashboard), then go to **Settings** → **Workspace**.

    Give the workspace a name your team will recognize, such as the client, brand, or market name. You can create and switch workspaces later when you need to separate access or data.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Set up Signals

Signals collects first-party conversion events from your website through a collection subdomain you control.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start your first signal">
    In the dashboard, open **Signals** and click **New signal** or **Set up your first signal**.

    Enter a collection subdomain that you control, such as `collect.example.com`. Do not include `https://`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the CNAME record">
    Copy the CNAME name and target from PlainRouter into your DNS provider. Keep the setup screen open or return to it from **Signals**.

    CNAME detection and automatic HTTPS validation can take several minutes. If setup remains pending, click **Retry validation** after your provider has published the change.

    See [Configure DNS and CNAME](/signals/configure-dns) for provider field guidance and troubleshooting.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify server-side ingestion">
    When the collection domain becomes active, send the identity-free verification event through `POST /api/v1/verification-events` or call the MCP tool `verify_signal_ingestion`.

    PlainRouter completes setup after that event reaches the Signal ledger. The operation is idempotent and does not send data to Meta or create spend.

    See [Verify Signal ingestion](/signals/verify-ingestion) for both methods.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The browser pixel is optional for activation. Install it afterward if you want browser events, visitor joins, and managed browser/server deduplication. Only load it where your consent policy permits terminal storage and downstream advertising use.

<Note>
  Signal setup is optional and resumable. Closing the setup screen does not remove your collection domain or log you out.
</Note>

## Confirm your setup

Your initial PlainRouter setup is complete when:

- **Signals** shows setup as complete after receiving the server verification event on an active collection domain.
- Your workspace name clearly identifies the client, brand, or market it contains.

You can now return to the [dashboard](https://plainrouter.com/dashboard) and continue working from the workspace you created.
