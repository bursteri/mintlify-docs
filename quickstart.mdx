This guide connects your AI client to PlainRouter and authorizes access to one advertising account.

## Prerequisites

Before you begin, you need:

- A [PlainRouter account](https://plainrouter.com/register).
- A supported advertising account you are allowed to manage.
- Codex or another client that supports remote MCP servers and OAuth.

## Connect your client

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add the PlainRouter MCP server">
    In Codex, run:

    ```bash
    codex mcp add plainrouter --url https://plainrouter.com/mcp
    ```

    For another MCP client, add `https://plainrouter.com/mcp` as a remote server URL.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize access">
    Start the connection from your MCP client. PlainRouter opens a browser authorization page.

    Sign in, choose the advertising account you want the agent to use, and approve the requested access.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify the connection">
    Ask your agent to retrieve the connected advertising account context. A successful response identifies the account authorized for that connection.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  PlainRouter does not grant an agent access to every account in your workspace. Each authorization is scoped to the account you select.
</Tip>

## Next step

Open the [PlainRouter dashboard](https://plainrouter.com/dashboard) to configure your workspace, connect a destination, or install a first-party signal tracker.
