---
title: "Configure DNS and CNAME"
description: "Connect a first-party collection subdomain to PlainRouter and complete DNS validation."
---

PlainRouter collects Signals through a subdomain you control, such as `collect.example.com`. Connecting this collection domain requires a CNAME record and one or more TXT records.

The change applies only to the collection subdomain. It does not move your website, email, or root domain to PlainRouter.

## How the records work

| Record | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| `CNAME` | Routes the collection subdomain to PlainRouter. |
| `TXT` | Verifies domain ownership and enables HTTPS for the collection subdomain. |

<Warning>
  Add every record exactly as shown in PlainRouter. The names and values displayed in your setup are specific to your collection domain.
</Warning>

## Before you begin

You need:

- Access to the DNS provider that manages your website domain.
- Permission to add CNAME and TXT records.
- A collection subdomain that is not already used by another service.

You can identify the active DNS provider by checking where your domain's nameservers are managed. This may be different from the company where you registered the domain.

## Add the DNS records

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the DNS configuration">
    In PlainRouter, open **Signals** and continue setup.

    After you enter your collection domain, PlainRouter displays a table containing the exact **Type**, **Name**, and **Value** for each required record.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the TXT records">
    In your DNS provider, create each TXT record shown by PlainRouter.

    Copy each **Name** and **Value** separately. Do not add quotation marks unless your DNS provider adds them automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the CNAME record">
    Create the CNAME using the collection domain as its name and the PlainRouter target as its value.

    Enter only the hostname in the value field. Do not include `https://`, a path, or a trailing slash.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and validate">
    Save the records and return to PlainRouter.

    TXT records are typically validated within five minutes. If setup remains pending after five minutes, click **Retry validation**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Match your provider's fields

DNS providers use different labels for the same fields:

| PlainRouter | Other common labels | What to enter |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Type** | Record type | `CNAME` or `TXT` |
| **Name** | Host, hostname, alias | The record name shown in PlainRouter |
| **Value** | Target, points to, content | The record value shown in PlainRouter |
| TTL | Time to live | Use **Auto** or the provider default |

Some providers automatically append your root domain to the **Name** field. If entering `collect.example.com` produces `collect.example.com.example.com`, enter only the host portion, such as `collect`.

<Tip>
  Use the copy button beside each DNS name and value in PlainRouter. This avoids missing characters in verification tokens.
</Tip>

## Confirm activation

PlainRouter validates both ownership and HTTPS before activating the collection domain. Keep the setup screen open, or return later through **Signals**.

When validation succeeds, PlainRouter automatically moves setup to **Set up Signals pixel**. Keep the CNAME and TXT records in place after activation.

## Troubleshoot validation

### The CNAME does not validate

- Remove any existing `A`, `AAAA`, or CNAME record using the same collection hostname. A hostname cannot point to multiple services at once.
- Confirm the CNAME value contains only the target hostname shown in PlainRouter.
- Check that you edited the DNS zone used by your domain's active nameservers.
- If your DNS provider adds the root domain automatically, make sure the record name is not duplicated.

### The TXT records do not validate

- Confirm you added every TXT record shown in PlainRouter.
- Compare the complete name and value, including underscores, hyphens, and dots.
- Remove quotation marks you entered manually if your provider stores them as part of the value.
- Wait for cached DNS responses to expire, then click **Retry validation** again.

### HTTPS validation fails

HTTPS becomes available only after the ownership and certificate TXT records are visible publicly.

If PlainRouter reports a CAA conflict, review the CAA records on your domain and allow the certificate authority named in the validation error. If you do not manage those restrictions, ask your DNS administrator to update them.

### Setup is still pending

DNS updates can take longer than the provider's dashboard suggests. Leave the records in place and retry after several minutes. Changing the values repeatedly restarts the validation process.
