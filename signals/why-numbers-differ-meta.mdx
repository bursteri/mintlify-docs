---
title: "Why numbers differ from Meta clicks"
description: "Compare PlainRouter arrivals with Meta-claimed clicks, check consent and eligibility, and separate an observed difference from website drop-off."
---

Meta counts ad clicks. PlainRouter counts eligible observed arrivals in your workspace. A click does not guarantee an arrival, and an arrival does not require a Meta click. The difference is useful evidence to investigate, but it is not a count of lost visitors.

## What does each number mean?

| Number | Definition and scope |
| --- | --- |
| PlainRouter **Total arrivals** | The sum of eligible observed event counts in the workspace's hourly aggregates. It is not restricted to landing-page views or deduplicated into people or sessions. |
| **Platform-claimed clicks** | Meta's account-level `clicks` field, stored during scheduled reconciliation. It is not an outbound-click or landing-page-view count. |
| **Difference** | Total arrivals minus platform-claimed clicks. |
| **Difference vs claimed** | Difference divided by claimed clicks, multiplied by 100. Unavailable when claimed clicks are zero. |

Use the same click metric when checking Ads Manager. Meta distinguishes link clicks from other ad interactions; a link can also lead to a destination within Meta. See [Meta's explanation of link clicks](https://developers.meta.com/horizon/resources/optimize-ad-campaign/).

The panel covers the last seven complete days, shortened by retained history, using the connected Meta ad account's timezone where available. The displayed period is the comparison window. The workspace's arrivals can include other traffic sources, while the ad account can contain ads sending people to other websites.

## Which arrivals are eligible?

PlainRouter includes observed arrivals classified as valid and permitted by its customer-visible privacy rules. It excludes modeled rows, invalid traffic, and suppressed strict-EU passive cells. A strict-EU passive cell contributes only after it reaches five arrivals.

The channel table is a narrower consented subset. It requires a stored source and eligible consent and scope. Source-less, legitimate-interest, and severed-measurement rows do not become channel-attributed traffic. Do not add the channel rows and expect the workspace total. See [the panel's eligibility rules](/signals/health-and-performance#arrivals-vs-platform-claimed-clicks).

## How does consent change the comparison?

The ordinary Signals browser collector sends events only after `ad_storage`, `ad_user_data`, and `ad_personalization` are all granted. A visitor who leaves before that grant produces no ordinary browser event. Earlier queued events are not replayed after consent.

Separately collected passive arrivals may contribute to aggregate totals within the privacy boundary. They cannot identify a visitor or populate the channel table, and they never create destination deliveries. Their presence does not mean the browser collector runs without consent.

A click reported by Meta does not establish that the visitor later granted consent on your website. See [Track events and consent](/signals/track-events).

## What can neither side tell you?

| Side | Visibility limit |
| --- | --- |
| PlainRouter | Cannot independently observe a click inside Meta or identify which claimed click failed to become an arrival. A missing browser event cannot distinguish early exit, missing consent, blocked collection, or a broken installation by itself. |
| Meta click report | Cannot establish that your website loaded, PlainRouter received an eligible event, or the visitor completed a purchase. It does not contain your workspace's full arrival ledger. |

For example, 800 arrivals and 1,000 claimed clicks produce a difference of −200 and −20%. They do **not** establish that 200 people were lost: the totals have different units and populations. This is an illustrative calculation, not an expected discrepancy rate.

## How do I investigate a change?

1. Match the Meta ad account, exact click metric, complete dates, and timezone. Check whether that account advertises other domains.
2. Check the panel's status. **Unavailable** means missing, stale, or incomplete comparison evidence; it does not mean zero clicks. Opening the page or exporting CSV does not fetch fresh Meta data.
3. Check recent changes to consent, landing URLs, redirects, script coverage, and collection failures. Use [installation checks](/signals/install-pixel#verify-the-installation) for a controlled consented visit.
4. Keep arrival differences separate from [conversion reconciliation](/signals/health-and-performance#verified-gap). Neither difference alone proves fraud, incrementality, CPA, or wasted spend.

A useful outcome is a matched reporting window and an evidenced cause, or an explicitly unexplained remainder. Do not change consent or invent an adjustment factor to force equality.
