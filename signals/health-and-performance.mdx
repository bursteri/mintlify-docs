Open **Signals** to check collection status and compare arrivals with stored platform activity. Use **Last 7 days**, **Last 30 days**, or **Last 90 days** to select a completed reporting window. The dates and timezone shown above the metrics define the actual period; retention can shorten it. **Today so far** is a separate, incomplete local-day count.

Opening this page or choosing **Export** reads stored data. It does not trigger a live Meta Marketing API request.

You need an active Signal for arrival collection and a connected Meta destination for platform and delivery diagnostics. Missing platform history does not mean zero activity.

## Summary metrics

### Events received

The arrival total counts dedicated arrival records sent once per document load. A consented `PageView`, purchase, or other conversion does not add another arrival. Arrivals confirm collection; they never create Meta deliveries.

Arrivals stay aggregate-only and do not appear in event-level lists or detail APIs. Their records contain no page URL, referrer, platform or channel source, visitor identity, IP address, user agent, or commerce details. Event time is rounded down to the UTC hour. Invalid traffic, synthetic events, and legacy exposure rows do not enter the arrival total.

The total remains a complete number, including when it is below five. A strict-EU severed subtotal from one to four is withheld separately. In that case, the page and CSV say **Complete total; one small cell is withheld**. The `partial` marker refers to the withheld component; it does not subtract arrivals from the total or the difference calculation.

### Match quality

**Event Match Quality** shows a stored Meta score and its capture date when available. A missing score is not zero. Follow the displayed notice if capture is pending, Meta returns no score, or the connection needs `ads_read` access. Match quality alone does not prove successful delivery or improved advertising results.

## Arrivals vs platform-claimed clicks

The **Arrivals and platform activity** panel compares observed arrivals with account-level Meta data for the displayed period.

| Metric | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Last N days** | Complete arrival total for the retained completed-day window. |
| **Today so far** | Arrivals since the start of the current local day, separate from the completed window. |
| **Platform-claimed clicks** | Account-level clicks captured from Meta for the same period. |
| **Impressions** | Stored Meta impressions for the connected account. |
| **Platform spend** | Stored account spend in the displayed account currency. |
| **Difference** | Total arrivals minus platform-claimed clicks. |

These platform metrics describe the ad account, which may advertise more than this website. They do not match individual clicks to individual arrivals or establish attribution, incrementality, or return on ad spend.

### Platform read state

The panel shows the last platform read time and counts days in three states:

| State | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Reported** | A successful read stored a daily Meta row. |
| **Read but not reported** | A successful read covered the date, but Meta returned no row for it. |
| **Not yet read** | No qualifying successful read covers the date. |

A completed read with no reported row is different from a missing read. Missing or incomplete platform evidence stays unavailable; do not turn a dash into zero. An actual zero-spend result can show **This account reported no spend in this period**.

### Channel details and export

Channel details read consented, attributed conversion-event data separately from arrival totals. They include global consent rows and full-consent strict-EU rows. Portfolio-scoped, legitimate-interest, severed-measurement, and source-less rows do not appear in the channel view. Because arrival records carry no channel identity, channel rows do not allocate or sum to the arrival total.

Choose **Export** to download the stored comparison as CSV with its reporting period and total-basis labels. The export preserves the distinction between unavailable data and zero.

### Verified revenue

**Verified revenue** shows eligible settled revenue grouped by currency. Connect [Stripe verified revenue](/signals/track-events#verify-stripe-checkout-settlement) from workspace settings. Revenue, arrivals, and platform spend are different measurements; do not combine currencies or infer attribution from their proximity on the page.

## Why numbers differ

Plainrouter arrivals, ad clicks, pageviews, sessions and visitors count different things. A difference between them does not, by itself, measure lost visitors or failed conversion delivery.

### Meta clicks

Compare against the connected Meta ad account's `clicks` metric, not outbound clicks or landing-page views. Plainrouter arrivals can include traffic from other sources, and the ad account may advertise other websites. The totals do not pair individual clicks with individual arrivals.

Match the account, complete dates and timezone shown in the comparison. Check the stored data's availability before treating a missing value as zero. Keep this arrival comparison separate from [Verified gap](/signals/health-and-performance#verified-gap), which compares conversion counts.

### GA4

Choose one GA4 metric and record the report settings. Users and sessions are not arrival counts. For collection troubleshooting, compare web `page_view` events for the same domain, then check tag triggers, filters and consent settings. GA4 can record pageviews on browser-history changes as well as page loads; test these separately. See [Google's page-view definition](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/9216061).

### Plausible

Keep unique visitors, visits, pageviews and custom-event goals separate. Start with pageviews when checking collection, with the same website, dates, timezone and filters. Repeated pageviews need not represent different visitors. See [Plausible's metric definitions](https://plausible.io/docs/metrics-definitions).

### Check the collection difference

1. Match the metric, website and reporting period before comparing totals.
2. Check your [consent configuration](/signals/overview#consent-behavior) and any privacy-limited values shown by Plainrouter. Arrivals do not wait for consent; conversion events and the channel breakdown do. Keep these counts separate.
3. Test one page load and one in-app navigation with your actual consent choices. Confirm which requests reach each collector using the [installation checks](/signals/install-pixel#verify-the-installation).
4. Inspect duplicate tags, redirects and blocked requests. Record the settings and request outcomes needed to reproduce an unexplained change.

Compare GA4 and Plausible using their own reports alongside Plainrouter. The platform-claimed-clicks panel reads stored Meta data; it does not reconcile those analytics reports.

## Health states

Account-scoped health remains available through [`get_signal_health`](/mcp/tools#get_signal_health). These diagnostic states are separate from the Signals overview's collection status.

| State | Meaning | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Setup required** | Signals or the collection hostname is not active. | Finish installation or recover the hostname. |
| **Awaiting data** | Setup is active, but no retained arrival evidence exists. | Visit the installed website and check its arrival request. |
| **Quiet** | Retained arrivals exist, but none fall in the selected window or today. | Check current traffic and the selected dates before reinstalling. |
| **Measurement pending** | Arrival activity exists, but delivery, EMQ, or reconciliation evidence is incomplete. | Check eligible conversions and allow scheduled measurements to complete. |
| **Needs attention** | A warning or critical diagnostic is active. | Follow the returned diagnostic. |
| **Healthy** | Required recent measurements exist without active warnings. | Continue monitoring. |

## Delivery acceptance

The account-scoped health response reports the percentage of non-test destination deliveries from events received in the last 24 hours that reached an accepted state:

```text
acceptance rate = accepted deliveries / total non-test deliveries × 100
```

Pending includes queued, sent, and retrying deliveries. Inspect an individual conversion through [the event API](/api/conversions) or the [CLI](/cli/command-reference#events) to check its delivery trace.

## Verified gap

[`get_performance`](/mcp/tools#get_performance) reads stored reconciliation counts:

```text
observed gap = gateway-verified accepted conversions - Meta-reported conversions
```

A positive gap means the gateway count is higher; a negative gap means Meta's count is higher. Alignment uses the larger total as the denominator and is unavailable when both are zero. This is a conversion-count comparison, separate from arrivals versus clicks.

## Gateway vs Meta chart

The daily rows in the stored performance response identify gateway-verified conversions, Meta-reported conversions, and the observed gap for each report date. Use those rows or the [reconciliation report API](/api/conversions) when investigating a conversion-count difference. The Signals overview no longer presents the old gateway chart.

## Why is Event Match Quality blank?

**Event Match Quality** in Signals reads the latest stored Meta composite score for the workspace's active destination. A dash means the score is unavailable; it does not mean zero. Read the accompanying notice and UTC date before comparing scores.

| Display | Meaning and next step |
| --- | --- |
| A score and UTC date | A stored capture contains a Meta composite score. Check its date; opening Signals does not refresh it. |
| **Connect Meta to read Event Match Quality.** | No active Meta destination is available. [Connect the conversion destination](/signals/connect-meta#connect-the-destination). |
| **Reconnect Meta with ads_read to read Event Match Quality. Delivery is unchanged.** | The active connection lacks the required read access. Reconnect the intended Meta connection; inspect delivery separately. |
| **Pending first capture.** | No stored capture is available yet. This is different from Meta returning no scores. |
| **Meta returned no composite Event Match Quality scores.** | A capture completed without a usable composite score. Treat the score as unavailable and check the capture date. |

EMQ is match-quality evidence, not confirmation of an individual conversion's acceptance, attribution, or revenue impact. Use the [event delivery trace](/api/conversions#how-do-i-confirm-meta-accepted-the-event) to investigate a purchase and the [EMQ guide](https://plainrouter.com/library/meta-event-match-quality) for matching concepts.

## What does Meta recovery evidence show?

The **Meta recovery** panel groups eligible server-side Meta `Purchase` deliveries by UTC day and separates their browser-pixel observations. It includes non-test, accepted deliveries for consented, observed, joined, attributed purchase events. It does not count identity-free arrivals or every purchase recorded in Plainrouter.

Expand a day to inspect `event_id`, **Provenance**, `observed_browser`, and **Source**:

| Browser observation | Interpretation |
| --- | --- |
| **observed** | The snippet reported a browser-pixel observation. This is not a Meta attribution receipt. |
| **not observed** | The snippet reported no browser-pixel observation. Server delivery does not prove the browser event would otherwise have been lost. |
| **unknown** | No definite browser observation is available. Keep it separate from **not observed**. |

Daily totals can exceed the inspection list, which shows at most 100 events per day. These rows use UTC delivery-record dates; do not assume they align with the overview's local-day arrival windows.

For a disputed event, copy its non-secret `event_id`, open [its delivery trace](/api/conversions#how-do-i-confirm-meta-accepted-the-event), and compare the matching event in Meta Events Manager. Keep browser observation, server acceptance, and Meta reporting as separate evidence. An empty panel means no eligible delivery evidence is available for that period; it does not establish that the website had no purchases. These counts do not measure incremental conversions, recovered revenue, or a guaranteed performance improvement.

## Diagnostics

Use `get_signal_health` for account-scoped delivery, match-quality, and reconciliation diagnostics. An arrival proves collection but cannot satisfy every health check. A missing Meta destination, failed deliveries, missing snapshots, stale reports, and a reconciliation gap require different follow-up actions.

## Measurement freshness

Reconciliation normally runs daily. History older than 48 hours is stale. When `get_performance` returns `not_measurable`, its quantitative values cannot support an Actions proposal. Check the response's reporting period, data source, and generation time before using it.

## Troubleshoot by symptom

| Symptom | Check next |
| --- | --- |
| **Not receiving yet** | Confirm the deployed snippet sends an accepted `/v1/arrival` request, then wait for stored arrival evidence. |
| Previously installed, now quiet | Check the selected dates and current site traffic. A quiet window does not erase earlier installation evidence. |
| Collection hostname removed | Follow [managed-hostname recovery](/signals/configure-dns#cloudflare-removed-the-collection-hostname). |
| Arrivals appear but conversions do not reach Meta | Check consent, the destination, and the individual event's delivery trace. |
| Platform numbers are unavailable | Check connection permissions, the selected account, retained dates, and platform read states. |
| Gateway and Meta conversion counts differ | Review consent-skipped, pending or failed deliveries, late reporting, deduplication, and duplicate integrations. |

## Delete a Signal

The Signals overview no longer has the old health-header delete control. During collection-domain setup, workspace owners can use the domain's delete control to reset Signals. This removes its collection configuration and related data; accepted events already held by Meta are not retracted.

Deleting the entire workspace is a separate permanent action in workspace settings and also removes its linked accounts and workspace data.
