August 17, 2026

We shipped the first complete Launcher Drive foundation and moved the redesigned Signals ledger through staging into production.

## Drive folders are verified before sync

Launcher no longer trusts the first workspace to claim a Drive folder. A folder must prove ownership before inventory sync can run.

- A matching verified workspace email domain can verify a non-public folder.
- Cross-domain and owner-metadata-unavailable folders use a generated challenge file.
- Link-public folders always require the challenge-file path.
- Unverified claims expire after 72 hours, while verified cross-workspace conflicts fail closed.

## Drive sync gained durable receipts

New Drive work uses shared operation receipts for dispatch, retries, reconciliation, and final outcomes. Historical receipt records remain readable, while new work follows a versioned queue contract without ambiguous fallback behavior.

For users, this means sync work can be retried and audited without silently duplicating an operation or losing its original result.

## The Signals ledger was redesigned

The event ledger now uses a production-verified partitioned storage design with exact rollup coverage, bounded retention paths, replay-horizon protection, and privacy erasure that preserves the accounting spine while removing subject data.

The redesign passed fresh PostgreSQL deployment checks and was promoted from staging to production on the same release line.
