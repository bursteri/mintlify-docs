Customer-facing changes available in November. Read linked guides for prerequisites and verification.

<Update label="November 20, 2026">
  ## Sign in without a password

  **Changed** · Use Google or a ten-minute emailed sign-in link/code. Sensitive actions use a fresh email code or your enabled authenticator/recovery method. Account passwords and GitHub sign-in are removed. [Secure your account](/reference/account-security).
</Update>

<Update label="November 19, 2026">
  ## Author Automations from your agent

  **Added** · Scoped MCP tools can inspect, save, revise, enable and stop scheduled automations. Ask refuses agent authoring; Custom checks explicit permissions and Full permits admitted authoring. Enabled runs retain their separate authorization across mode changes. [Use Automations](/actions/automations).

  ## Connect report tables with narrower credentials

  **Added** · Issue a Reporting Workspace key through the management API, select from its authorized workspaces and read flat channel, monthly-statement or fixed-window arrivals rows. Keep currencies, missing values and observation times with your charts. [Set up Reporting access](/api/reporting).

  ## Review your account and team security history

  **Added** · Security log brings your own activity into one page; team owners can filter team events and export CSV. Security emails also notify supported account and team access changes. [Inspect security history](/reference/account-security#where-can-i-inspect-sign-ins-and-security-changes).
</Update>

<Update label="November 18, 2026">
  ## Schedule checks from Automations

  **Added** · Owners and admins can save account-local schedules and conditions for supported advertising actions or reports, inspect check results and receive owner alert emails. Copies begin paused and resulting changes still need receipt verification. [Create an automation](/actions/automations#create-an-automation-in-the-dashboard).

  ## Download the whole attribution record

  **Added** · Export a complete JSON file of retained reporting, Meta observations, receipts and audit evidence, with a section manifest and hashes. Scoped read grants, complete-file limits and each section's actual reporting window remain explicit. [Download a record](/signals/records-and-statements#download-a-whole-attribution-record).
</Update>

<Update label="November 17, 2026">
  ## Keep frozen monthly statements

  **Added** · Eligible paid-plan months freeze after the reporting wait, preserving counted totals, conversion definitions and separate Meta claims with coverage and hashes. Historical retention and later Meta blanking remain visible. [Read monthly statements](/signals/records-and-statements).
</Update>

<Update label="November 14, 2026">
  ## Inspect revisions to Meta's past-day totals

  **Added** · Inventory metrics can include retained revisions when a previously read day's account totals change. First reads and incomplete days remain separate, and capped results require a narrower range. [Read metric revisions](/actions/account-inventory#inspect-revisions-to-past-day-meta-totals).
</Update>

<Update label="November 13, 2026">
  ## Keep reporting coverage with the answer

  **Added** · Journey, channel-cost, arrivals and inventory-metrics reads now explain retained starts, unread and revisable Meta dates, activity gaps and the separate untagged-spend window. REST exports carry the same limits; unknown evidence stays unknown. [Interpret attribution coverage](/signals/conversion-journeys#how-do-i-read-attribution-coverage).
</Update>

<Update label="November 12, 2026">
  ## Inspect Meta account changes in Audit log

  **Added** · Compare retained Meta activity by source and object level, inspect available before/after details and follow a matching Plainrouter receipt. Gap notices and missing receipts remain visible; provider observations are separate from the workspace audit chain. [Use Meta account changes](/actions/audit-log#inspect-meta-account-changes).

  ## Review large first launches before they spend

  **Changed** · In permission-based Custom mode, a first activation of a Plainrouter-created campaign or ad set can require person approval when its budget exceeds the launch limit or comparable evidence is unavailable. Full keeps its separate admitted-action behavior. [Read launch-budget limits](/actions/policies-and-safety#what-happens-when-an-agent-turns-on-a-new-campaign-or-ad-set).
</Update>

<Update label="November 11, 2026">
  ## Choose how agents may act

  **Changed** · Agent rules now offers Ask, Custom and Full. Ask waits for a person; Custom permits selected agent action types; Full permits admitted agent changes without individual approval, including supported budget raises and activation. Authorization, policy blocks and provider verification still apply. [Configure Agent rules](/actions/policies-and-safety).

  ## Export and verify audit evidence through REST

  **Added** · Scoped Read Workspace keys can page through redacted audit events and check the current stored hash chain. Save prior heads separately when comparing snapshots: a valid current chain is not independent proof against tail deletion. [Read export and verification limits](/api/report-exports).

  ## Confirm email changes before switching access

  **Changed** · Your current address and access stay active while a new address is pending. Password accounts confirm with their current password; accounts without one first approve the change from their current mailbox. Confirm the new address to finish. [Change your email](/reference/account-security#how-do-i-change-my-email-without-losing-access).

  ## Replace idle write credentials

  **Changed** · Workspace Write and legacy Admin keys unused for 90 days are revoked on their next authentication, including keys set to Never expire. Read and Management keys are outside this idle rule. [Check key lifecycle](/mcp/workspace-tokens#does-never-prevent-every-kind-of-revocation).
</Update>

<Update label="November 10, 2026">
  ## Compare Campaigns and inspect Meta changes

  **Changed** · Campaigns brings campaigns, ad sets and ads into one retained-data table with account-local date comparisons, on/off controls and daily-budget editing. Inventory metrics can also expose retained Meta activity with source and history-gap details. Provider observations and a changed display remain separate from verified action receipts. [Use Campaigns and activity reads](/actions/account-inventory).

  ## Read Stripe subscription revenue

  **Added** · Connect a restricted read-only Stripe key to inspect a timestamped current-subscription snapshot, per-currency MRR, trials, past-due subscriptions and unknown amounts. Signed webhooks keep it current; this is separate from payment attribution and historical conversion import. The former history importer has been removed. [Configure and interpret the snapshot](/signals/track-events#read-current-subscription-revenue).

  ## Export journeys, channel costs and arrivals

  **Added** · Read the existing scoped reporting evidence through JSON REST endpoints with a Read Workspace key. Preserve coverage, basis notes and missing values with the export. [Use reporting exports](/api/report-exports).
</Update>

<Update label="November 9, 2026">
  ## Inspect ad-tag coverage

  **Added** · Inventory reads expose Plainrouter tag states and retained seven-day spend coverage, with incomplete evidence kept unavailable. The checker does not modify ads or tags. [Interpret coverage](/actions/account-inventory#check-plainrouter-ad-tag-coverage).
</Update>

<Update label="November 4, 2026">
  ## Save Dashboard views

  **Added** · Create personal or shared Dashboard views and configure widgets when your role permits editing. Today keeps partial-day measurements visible; Meta Purchase cards use their separate daily comparison. Older Performance and Operations links redirect to Dashboard or Signals. [Use saved views](/signals/health-and-performance#save-a-dashboard-view).

  ## Choose one business conversion

  **Changed** · Select one workspace conversion event, including Sign up or Demo booked. Changes require acknowledgment and password confirmation because they affect past and future reporting. Site installation, consent controls and the Server secret now live with Signals. [Choose the conversion event](/signals/conversions).

  ## Create keys without a calendar expiry

  **Added** · Workspace and Management key creation supports **Never** under Expires. Revocation, role changes and other lifecycle checks still apply. [Choose a key expiry](/mcp/workspace-tokens).
</Update>
