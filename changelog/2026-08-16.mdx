August 16, 2026

We shipped owner-managed workspace tokens for MCP clients and closed the audit gap around workspace policy changes.

## MCP clients can use workspace-scoped tokens

Workspace owners can issue a fixed bearer credential when an MCP client or automation cannot complete interactive OAuth.

Every token is bound to one workspace and one active Meta ad account. Owners choose a Read, Write, or Admin tier and a 30, 90, or 365 day expiry. Tokens can be rotated or revoked without widening their original account scope.

PlainRouter shows the plaintext token only once. Lifecycle actions are recorded in the durable audit chain without storing the credential itself.

## Policy changes now enter the audit chain

Changes to workspace action policy are now recorded atomically with the policy update. The audit record uses the same recomputable v2 chain as proposals, approvals, executions, and token lifecycle events.

This closes a visibility gap: an operator can now trace both the action decision and the policy posture that governed it without relying on a mutable application log.
