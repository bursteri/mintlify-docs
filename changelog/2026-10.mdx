Customer-facing changes available in October. Read linked guides for prerequisites and verification.

<Update label="October 12, 2026">
  ## Inspect Actions with a Read key

  **Improved** · Newly issued Read keys can inspect action records, policy and decision receipts. Existing proposal grants remain compatible for reads; older Read keys need fresh authorization for the new operation. Previews and proposals still require Write. [Check key permissions](/mcp/workspace-tokens#access-tiers).

  ## Keep protected changes under person approval

  **Improved** · Budget increases and campaign/ad-set resumes require a person approval even in Full. One protected action protects the containing batch. Retired-mode proposals cannot be promoted by changing today's policy. [Review protected changes](/actions/policies-and-safety#which-changes-still-need-a-person-in-full).

  ## Review frozen target evidence

  **New** · Supported target proposals preserve a seven-completed-day comparison of Meta figures, counted arrivals and eligible purchases for Inbox, API/MCP reads and decision receipts. Missing values remain unknown; the snapshot does not authorize spend. [Interpret the comparison](/actions/review-proposals#what-is-the-frozen-counted-evidence).

  ## Reconcile purchases with shared order keys

  **Improved** · Browser, backend and verified Stripe observations can reconcile against the same explicit workspace order key. Keep event IDs stable for retries and align Session metadata when sources describe one order. [Set up order keys](/api/conversions#how-do-shared-order-ids-reconcile-purchases).

  ## Verify renewals and refund status

  **Improved** · Eligible global-policy subscription renewals can retain prior acquisition context. Stripe setup lists renewal and refund-status events, with an unverified-status notice when only charge summaries were observed. [Update Stripe webhook subscriptions](/signals/track-events#configure-the-webhook).

  ## Keep Analytics and Marketing choices separate

  **Improved** · The current v4 card records Analytics independently from the three advertising permissions controlled by Marketing. Saved older choices retain their original meaning; recorded choices can be inspected in the consent report. [Verify consent choices](/signals/track-events#how-does-the-snippet-choose-a-consent-source).

  ## Upgrade generated clients

  **Updated** · TypeScript, Python and CLI `0.9.0`, Ruby `0.5.0`, and PHP `0.3.0` refresh the signed-contract models. Go remains `v0.6.0`; package upgrades do not expand credential access. [Choose a client](/sdk/overview#what-changed-in-the-current-releases).
</Update>

<Update label="October 10, 2026">
  ## Use one key across selected workspaces

  **New** · Choose a fixed workspace set when creating a key. MCP clients can list their authorized workspaces and select one per call; each workspace keeps its own role-capped grant. [Create and verify a workspace key](/mcp/workspace-tokens).

  ## Inspect cookies before consent

  **New** · Review pre-consent cookies, storage and tracker requests, follow available fix guidance, and rescan after changes. Embed a metadata-only declaration from the latest completed scan. Both are available on every plan. [Run a cookie scan](/signals/cookie-scan).

  ## Compare paid-account performance in Dashboard

  **New** · Inspect Meta spend, clicks, purchases and ROAS beside tagged-ad arrivals and eligible observed purchases. Coverage notes distinguish tagged ads from total account spend. Signals keeps setup, health and destination controls. [Interpret the account view](/signals/health-and-performance#how-do-i-read-paid-account-performance).

  ## Follow proposals in the updated Inbox

  **Improved** · Review reasoning, policy and activity in the detail pane, with technical fields under All details. Groups distinguish In progress, Done, Approved, not sent, and Stopped or failed. Approval alone still does not confirm a Meta change. [Review proposals](/actions/review-proposals).
</Update>

<Update label="October 6, 2026">
  ## Inspect rule runs and resume after review

  **Improved** · Account rules show recent windows, account totals and target decisions in Action policy, with links to available proposals. After inspecting two lost outcomes, the team owner can explicitly resume a suspended rule. Only actions judged after resumption count toward a new suspension. [Review runs and resume safely](/actions/account-rules).

  ## Follow inverse proposals for rule-made budget changes

  **Improved** · An eligible lost daily-budget change made by an account rule can propose a separate inverse to its recorded prior budget. Current-state checks and Ask/Full still govern that proposal; inverse proposals do not produce another inverse chain. [Inspect eligibility and verification](/actions/review-proposals#does-a-lost-outcome-create-an-inverse-proposal).

  ## Recover missing outcome follow-ups

  **Fixed** · Completed management actions can recover overdue outcome checks without repeating the original Meta write. Expired checks finish as unavailable rather than implying a performance verdict, while verified execution remains recorded. [Follow a delayed outcome](/actions/review-proposals#what-if-a-completed-action-has-no-later-outcome).
</Update>

<Update label="October 5, 2026">
  ## Define account performance rules

  **New** · Create disabled rules in Action policy or through MCP, then explicitly enable counted-arrival cost conditions for governed pauses and daily-budget changes. Inspect revisions and evaluation results. Rule-generated budget increases always need human approval, including in Full. [Set up account rules](/actions/account-rules).

  ## Follow inverse proposals after a lost outcome

  **New** · An eligible lost Resume or budget-change outcome can create a separate inverse proposal using the recorded prior state. Current-state checks protect intervening changes; Ask/Full and provider verification still apply. Inverse proposals do not create another inverse chain. [Inspect the proposed response](/actions/review-proposals#does-a-lost-outcome-create-an-inverse-proposal).

  ## Wait for completed-day metrics before judging an outcome

  **Improved** · Outcome checks require retained metrics fetched after the compared day completed. Missing or stale recent metrics can request a bounded refresh; unavailable data stays unknown rather than becoming zero. [Read the freshness requirements](/actions/review-proposals#how-are-later-performance-outcomes-checked).
</Update>

<Update label="October 4, 2026">
  ## Compare Meta metrics with counted arrivals

  **New** · API and MCP inventory reads include daily counted arrivals beside stored Meta metrics for campaigns, ad sets and ads. Ad rows can also show receipt-backed creative provenance when available. Unknown values stay distinct from zero. [Read the comparison](/actions/account-inventory#how-do-counted-arrivals-compare-with-meta-metrics).

  ## Review later outcomes in Inbox

  **Improved** · Verified resumes and budget changes show a comparison of counted arrivals and spend over completed account-local days, with the loss threshold and an eligible held/lost verdict. Unavailable data gets bounded retries. A verdict does not automatically undo the action. [Interpret the result](/actions/review-proposals#how-are-later-performance-outcomes-checked).

  ## Restore Meta access to the existing destination

  **Fixed** · Reconnecting the same Meta ad account after revocation or disconnection reuses its existing destination binding. Confirm the restored grant and check any failed delivery separately. [Reconnect Meta](/signals/connect-meta#reconnect-after-access-is-revoked-or-disconnected).
</Update>

<Update label="October 2, 2026">
  ## Review daily budgets and resumes in Campaigns

  **Improved** · Available daily-budget controls show the retained amount, proposed change and sync time before submission. Resume also asks you to confirm the current budget. Inputs and displays use the account currency's Meta units. [Use Campaigns controls](/actions/account-inventory).

  ## Recover interrupted Actions without duplicate writes

  **Fixed** · Approved batches can recover from a lost execution job, and stalled execution closes against its recorded receipts. Partial or uncertain Meta results remain visible for review. [Follow recovery](/actions/review-proposals#what-if-an-approved-batch-stalls).

  ## Use the Signals fallback consent card

  **New** · The v4 snippet can use supported external consent signals or a configured Plainrouter card with Accept all, Reject all and Customize. Partial choices keep advertising events silent until all three required permissions are granted. [Choose and test consent](/signals/track-events#how-does-the-snippet-choose-a-consent-source).

  ## Understand traffic context on arrivals

  **Improved** · Current v4 arrivals retain bounded page, campaign, referrer-host and coarse environment dimensions without visitor identifiers. They remain separate from consented conversion events and Meta delivery. [Review retained fields](/signals/install-pixel#which-arrival-fields-are-retained).

  ## Integrate from PHP with the published SDK

  **New** · Composer package `plainrouter/sdk` `0.1.0` provides event, operations and sandbox clients for PHP 8.2 and newer. [Install and verify the PHP SDK](/sdk/php).
</Update>
