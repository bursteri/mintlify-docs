PlainRouter gives AI agents secure, permissioned access to advertising accounts through the Model Context Protocol (MCP). It also collects first-party conversion signals and delivers them to connected destinations.

Use PlainRouter to:

- Connect Codex, Claude, and other MCP-compatible clients to an advertising account.
- Keep account access behind a browser-based authorization flow.
- Collect first-party conversion events through your own collection hostname.
- Send conversion signals to supported advertising platforms.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect your account">
    Sign in to PlainRouter and connect a supported advertising account through OAuth.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize your agent">
    Add the PlainRouter MCP server to your client. The browser authorization flow lets you choose which account the agent can access.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Route conversion signals">
    Configure a signal tracker to collect browser events and deliver approved conversions to your connected destination.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  PlainRouter limits each agent connection to the account and permissions you authorize. You can revoke access from your dashboard.
</Note>
