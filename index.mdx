Welcome to the PlainRouter help center. These guides help you collect first-party conversion signals, understand measurement health, and review evidence-backed actions proposed by an AI agent.

If this is your first visit, begin with the Quickstart. It takes you from account creation to an active Signals pixel. You can then connect Meta and an MCP-compatible agent when you are ready.
