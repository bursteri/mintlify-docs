Welcome to the PlainRouter help center. These guides help you collect first-party conversion signals, connect a verified Drive creative library, and govern evidence-backed actions proposed by an AI agent.

If this is your first visit, begin with the Quickstart. It takes you from account creation to verified server-side Signals ingestion. You can then add the optional browser pixel, connect Meta, and authorize an MCP-compatible agent when you are ready.

## Start free with self-serve onboarding

PlainRouter includes the complete control plane for $0 while combined monthly managed ad spend is under $300. The Free tier is not a time-limited trial. Registration and production-token management are self-serve.
