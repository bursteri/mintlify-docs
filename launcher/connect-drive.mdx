Legacy Launcher's Google Drive connection and inventory sync are retired. Folder claims, ownership verification, sync retries and connected-folder routes have been removed. Enabling a feature flag or issuing a new Workspace key cannot restore them.

## What should I use instead?

Use the supported [creative workflow](/actions/creative-workflow) to read the selected Meta ad account's creative library, stage a JPEG or PNG, or propose a paused ad copy. Uploaded-file and vetted-URL creative intake retain their own workspace availability and permission checks. Drive sharing does not authorize those operations.

The related [draft-file import](/launcher/import-draft-batch) and `launcher.draft_batch`, `launcher.batch_status` and `launcher.preview_batch` MCP tools are also removed. An older SDK method or stored grant does not make a retired route available.

## How should I interpret old records?

Historical folder-ownership audit events describe past activity. They do not establish a current Drive connection, fresh inventory or permission to execute a provider change. Inspect the relevant [Actions receipts and audit history](/actions/audit-log) for a recorded advertising operation.

A proposal still follows Ask or Full, current authorization, protected approvals and exact provider verification. A draft or preview is not Landed.
