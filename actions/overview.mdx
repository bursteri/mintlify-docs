title: "Actions overview"

---

Actions is PlainRouter's governed recommendation workflow. An authorized AI agent can read the approved account's Signals context, explain a proposed change, and place it in your approval queue.

<Warning>
  The current release is strictly **Suggest only**. Approving a recommendation records approval as `approved_without_execution`. PlainRouter does not write the change to Meta.
</Warning>

## The review loop

<Steps>
  <Step title="A person authorizes one account">
    During MCP authorization, you choose the exact advertising account the agent may access. PlainRouter binds the connection to that account rather than accepting an account ID from the agent.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The agent reads Signals context">
    The agent starts with account state, then reads Signal health and stored performance when relevant. All three reads carry the same workspace, account, destination, and dataset scope.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The agent proposes a batch">
    Each batch includes one or more typed actions, a plain-language rationale, exact evidence fields, a stable idempotency key, and a human-supplied target.
  </Step>
  <Step title="PlainRouter applies policy">
    Static safeguards can block the proposal. Checks that require unavailable live account or target state are labeled unavailable rather than guessed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="A person decides">
    A pending proposal appears in **Approval queue**. You can approve it or reject it with an optional reason.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The decision becomes audit history">
    PlainRouter records the proposal, policy result, evidence provenance, and human decision in a tamper-evident audit chain.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Supported proposal types

| Action | Target | Required parameters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Adjust budget | Ad account, campaign, ad set, or ad | New daily budget in the account currency's minor unit. |
| Set status | Ad account, campaign, ad set, or ad | `active` or `paused`. |
| Duplicate ad with creative | Ad | Creative-library asset reference and required initial `paused` status. |
| Upload asset | Ad account, campaign, ad set, or ad | Durable staged asset reference, image or video type, filename, and SHA-256 digest. |

These are proposal shapes. Their presence in a recommendation does not mean PlainRouter executed the action.

## Evidence boundaries

The current Signals reads provide aggregate account and dataset evidence:

- Authorized account, workspace, connection, Signal, and destination identity.
- First-party event activity and diagnostics.
- Meta delivery outcomes.
- Stored Event Match Quality snapshots.
- Stored gateway-versus-Meta conversion reconciliation.

They do not provide live Marketing API state for budget, spend, campaign status, learning phase, or target-level performance.

<Note>
  A human-supplied campaign, ad set, or ad ID establishes only which target the recommendation refers to. It does not prove that target's current state.
</Note>

## Suggest-only decisions

| Decision | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Approve | The batch and its actions become **Approved without execution**. No execution job is dispatched. |
| Reject | The batch and its actions become **Rejected**. The optional reason is recorded. |
| Policy block | The batch becomes **Blocked** and does not enter the pending review flow. |

Approved-without-execution recommendations do not increase the **Landed** count.
