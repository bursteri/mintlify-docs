Open **Inbox** from the Plainrouter sidebar to review agent proposals for the current workspace. Select an action to read its reasoning, target, policy result, and status. **Approve** and **Reject** decide the containing batch, including its other actions.

## Before you decide

Sign in with a browser session and workspace access. Approval requires the workspace's human approval permission; a workspace token cannot make this decision for you. Have the complete proposal available, including its account scope, parameters, evidence, and frozen `execution_mode_snapshot`.

The Inbox header shows the workspace's **current** policy mode. Do not substitute it for an older proposal's mode snapshot. A policy change does not turn a suggest-only proposal into executable work. For a complete example, follow [Create a paused Meta ad variant](/actions/creative-workflow#example-propose-a-paused-creative-variant).

## Read the Inbox groups

Counts represent actions, grouped into batches:

| Group | What appears here |
| --- | --- |
| **Needs your decision** | Pending actions awaiting a human decision. |
| **Awaiting verification** | Executing, executed-pending-verification, compensating, or measuring actions. These states do not establish a verified production outcome. |
| **Landed** | Verified actions with a verified execution receipt, **and** suggest-only recommendations recorded as **Approved without execution**. |
| **Drifted or failed** | Rejected, blocked, failed, uncertain, or rolled-back actions, plus actions without the required verified receipt. Read the exact status and reason. |

<Warning>
  The **Landed** group alone is not proof of a provider change. **Approved without execution** means no Meta write occurred. For production execution acceptance, every action in the batch needs a durable receipt and exact provider verification.
</Warning>

## Review the selected action

### Proposer, target, and reasoning

Read **Actor**, **Objects**, and **Agent reasoning**. Compare the complete proposal with the intended Meta ad account and platform-native target IDs. For creative duplication, `target_entity.id` identifies the source ad; the new ad ID exists only in the execution receipt after a provider write.

Confirm the person intended each source ad and asset selection. If the detail pane does not provide enough information to verify the account, mode, evidence, or parameters, check the original proposal before approving. Review every action in the containing batch.

### Evidence and policy checks

Read **Policy check**, including the decision, reasons, and any unavailable dynamic fields. Executable work fails closed when a required provider or policy field is unknown. Suggest-only recommendations can disclose unavailable state because approval cannot execute them.

The proposal's evidence must support its claims:

- `get-creative-library` provenance establishes selected-asset identity, approved-account ownership, readiness, and status.
- `staged-asset-manifest` provenance identifies the frozen bytes, SHA-256 digest, and detected content type for an upload.
- Creative performance is excluded from the durable action-bound citation. Human-supplied targets and aggregate Signals data are not current provider state.

## Approve a batch

The button is labeled **Approve**. Read the proposal's frozen mode and supported action before clicking; the button label does not describe the execution consequence.

| Proposal scope | Result of approval |
| --- | --- |
| **Suggest only** | Records **Approved without execution**. No Meta write occurs. |
| Supported executable creative proposal | Authorizes the batch and queues a fresh policy check before execution. It does not guarantee a successful provider write. |
| Closed one-run measurement | Starts a diagnostic run that remains **Not Landed** and requires verified compensation. It is not general production execution. |

For an ad copy, confirm `status: paused`. The supported creative workflow cannot activate the copy. Approval is authoritative once and does not bypass [execution-time policy](/actions/policies-and-safety#two-policy-gates).

### How do I verify the outcome?

Follow the action's status in Inbox, then use [Audit and its JSON export](/actions/audit-log) to trace the batch decision, receipt evidence, provider observations, and terminal verification.

When an action has a stored decision receipt, use **Audit → Download decision receipt** to inspect its terminal status and verification result. See [receipt fields and access](/actions/audit-log#download-an-action-decision-receipt).

**Approved**, **Executing**, and **Executed pending verification** do not mean Landed. A paused copy that Meta reports as `IN_PROCESS` still needs scheduled receipt-addressed verification. Inspect the exported events for progress or failure; do not create another copy to bypass a pending result. Escalate a `rollback_incomplete` result rather than treating it as restored.

## Reject a batch

Click **Reject**, optionally explain what the agent should reconsider, and confirm **Reject proposal**. The reason is stored in the audit event. Like approval, rejection applies to the containing batch.

Approval and rejection are idempotent terminal decisions for a pending batch. A second decision does not reopen it.

## Troubleshoot a missing decision or unexpected status

- **The proposal is missing:** confirm the selected workspace and proposal or batch ID. Use the returned `inbox_url`; `approval_queue_url` remains a compatibility alias for the same Inbox link.
- **No decision control is available:** check whether the batch is still pending. A completed decision is not reopened from Inbox.
- **The decision is forbidden:** sign in with an authorized human account in the correct workspace. A bearer token is not a substitute for this browser session.
- **An action is blocked:** read its exact reason and the [policy constraints](/actions/policies-and-safety) before resubmitting.
- **A suggest-only approval appears under Landed:** verify `approved_without_execution`. No provider object or execution receipt should be inferred from the group label.
