title: "Review action proposals"
description: "Evaluate evidence, policy checks, and action details in the approval queue."
---

Open **Approval queue** from the PlainRouter sidebar to review agent recommendations for the current workspace.

## Read the queue summary

The top of the page shows the policy mode and three counts:

| Count | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Waiting** | Batches still awaiting a human decision. |
| **Landed** | Batches that completed real execution in an execution-enabled release. Suggest-only approvals are excluded. |
| **Blocked** | Batches stopped by policy or execution safety checks. |

Use **Pending** for decisions that need you. Use **History** for approved, rejected, blocked, failed, completed, and rolled-back outcomes.

## Review a proposal card

Read each section before deciding:

### Proposer and account

The header identifies the agent, advertising account, proposal time, and current batch status. Confirm the account is the one you intended to authorize.

### Why now

This is the agent's plain-language rationale for treating the actions as one batch. It should describe the observed condition, the proposed response, and the expected outcome without claiming unavailable target state.

### Evidence provenance

Each cited source shows:

- The source tool.
- Account or dataset scope.
- Exact response fields used.
- When the snapshot was observed.
- The reporting window, when applicable.
- Whether stored performance history was measurable.

The target notice reminds you that the target was supplied by a person for selection only. Current target state remains unavailable in this release.

<Warning>
  Do not approve a rationale that presents aggregate conversion counts as campaign CPA, spend, budget state, learning phase, or target-level performance.
</Warning>

### Proposed actions

Each item identifies the action type, target type, target ID or name, important parameters, and its rationale. Budget values appear in the connected account's currency.

### Policy results

Static policy failures appear as blocking reasons. Checks that require live account or target state appear as **Dynamic checks unavailable** with the missing field names.

In **Suggest only**, unavailable dynamic checks require human review and never authorize execution.

## Approve a recommendation

<Steps>
  <Step title="Confirm scope and evidence">
    Verify the advertising account, dataset scope, observation times, and cited fields.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the action details">
    Review every target and parameter in the batch. Treat the proposal as one decision.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve">
    On desktop, click **Approve**. On mobile, use the approval control shown on the card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the terminal status">
    In the current release, the batch moves to **Approved without execution**. No Meta change occurs.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Reject a recommendation

Click **Reject**, optionally explain what the agent should reconsider, and confirm **Reject proposal**. The reason can help future agent reasoning and is stored with the audit event.

Approval and rejection are idempotent terminal decisions. A second decision does not reopen or execute the batch.

## Review checklist

- Is this the correct advertising account?
- Is every target the target a person intended?
- Are the evidence fields actually present in the named read tool?
- Is reconciliation recent and marked measurable before quantitative claims are used?
- Does the rationale avoid target-level claims that Signals cannot support?
- Are any dynamic checks unavailable?
- Does a static policy reason block the batch?
- Do you understand that current approval records agreement but does not execute?
